03 / CONTINUAL ADAPTATION

BCMoE: Expert Lifecycle Management for Budget-Constrained Continual Adaptation

Continual adaptation under a trainable-parameter budget, managing the MoE expert lifecycle through rerouting, growth in task-sensitive layers, and capacity recycling.

Read full abstract

Modern multimedia platforms and interactive systems face non-stationary data streams whose distributions evolve over time, making continual adaptation essential. In practice, however, adaptation is often constrained by a strict budget on trainable parameters. Under such constraints, the model must determine whether existing capacity is sufficient, when additional capacity is required, and how adaptation can continue once further growth is no longer feasible. To address this problem, we propose BCMoE, a budget-aware continual adaptation framework built on a frozen pretrained Transformer with lightweight MoE branches. BCMoE performs stage-wise adaptation through three actions. At each stage, it first probes whether rerouting to existing experts is sufficient for the incoming distribution shift. When reuse is not enough, it expands experts only in a small set of task-sensitive layers. Once further growth is limited by the budget or by structural expert slots, it recycles capacity by merging low-utility experts and distilling their functionality from cached representations. Experiments on two representative non-stationary streaming settings, namely stage-wise domain shift and mixture-proportion shift, show that BCMoE achieves the best overall performance under the same budget upper bound. Further analysis indicates that these gains come from stage-wise choices over rerouting, expert growth, and capacity recycling, highlighting the importance of lifecycle management for adaptation under fixed trainable capacity.

Under review at ICASSP
